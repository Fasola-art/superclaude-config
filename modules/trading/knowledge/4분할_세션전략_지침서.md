## 1. Executive Summary

Markets move based on **quartile price levels (25%, 50%, 75%)** and **session-based liquidity**, not randomness.

- Identify real-time trends and mean reversion points with **M1 (1-minute) multi-EMA**
- Confirm institutional trends with **Open Interest (OI)** + **Liquidation Map (TD indicator)**
- **Principle**: Longs are slow but shorts are fast → React more quickly in downtrends

## 3. Market Analysis: Structure and Institutional Logic

### Liquidity Hunting
- Institutions drive price to **liquidation pools** to fill orders
- 100x leverage zones are primary targets

### Mean Reversion
- When price moves excessively from long-term EMA (2880) → **Reversion to 360 EMA**

### Volatility Triggers
```
Pattern analysis: 70%
News: 30% (triggers major volatility)
Pattern breakdown = New trend initiation
```

### Data Asymmetry
- ❌ Retail exchange data (manipulation risk HIGH)
- ✅ **CME data** (CFTC regulated, reliability HIGH)
- CME Gap: Weekend price difference → Tendency to fill on Monday

## 4. [Bitcoin] Practical Trading Strategies

### Strategy 1: Short Position Attack (Primary)

**Entry Conditions**:
1. M1 EMAs in **bearish alignment**
2. Price rebounds to **previous range 75%** or **360/720 EMA resistance**

**Confirmation Signals**:
- **OI decreasing** while price rises
- → Interpret as fake bounce (short liquidation exhaustion)
- → **Enter short aggressively**

**Exit**:
- Reach lower long liquidation pool (100x leverage zone)
- Or full profit at previous low (0%)

### Strategy 2: Bullish Alignment Pullback Buy (Long)

**Entry Conditions**:
1. 360/720 EMA in **bullish alignment**
2. Price pulls back to **previous range 25%**
3. Enter after support confirmation

**Targets**:
- Next quartile level (50%, 75%)
- Exit after confirming upper short liquidation pool
